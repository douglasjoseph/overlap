How Overlap works

Overlap finds the people in your photos, on your phone. When you connect with a friend, the photos you took of them can go to them, and the photos they took of you come to you, grouped into moments by day and place.

Common questions

How do I connect with a friend?
Tap someone in "Who's in your photos?", say who they are by picking them from your contacts, then connect or send an invite. You're connected once they accept.

Who sees the photos I share?
Only the friend you share them with.

Can I choose what gets sent?
Yes. The first time, you review every photo of a friend before anything is sent. After that, you decide whether new photos of them go automatically (auto-share) or wait for your review. You can change this any time on their page.

I sent a photo by mistake.
Open it and tap Unshare. That works until your friend saves it to their Photos library.

A photo was matched to the wrong person.
Open the photo from that person's page and tap Not followed by their name (for example "Not Kaya"). Overlap stops grouping that face with them.

Why are some photos slow to appear?
Photos stored in iCloud are downloaded and checked in full before they can be shared, which happens on Wi-Fi. Full-size photos also upload only on Wi-Fi, and keep going after you leave the app.

How do I delete my account?
In Overlap, tap your initials at the top right, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, selfies, face print, connections and shared photos. See the privacy policy for details.
