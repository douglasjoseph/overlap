Overlap

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Overlap helps friends trade the photos they take of each other. Your phone finds your friends in your photos, and with your OK, sends those photos to them. Their photos of you come back to you. This policy explains what Overlap collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version. Your photo library is checked on your phone, not on our servers. A photo leaves your phone only when you share it, and only the friend you share it with can see it. Your face print is shared only with friends you connect with. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from Settings in the app.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an identifier for your Apple account (and an email address only if you choose to share one). You add your mobile phone number, which we verify with a text message, and a display name.
  • Selfies and your face print. During setup, Overlap takes a few photos of your face with the front camera, only after you agree. Your phone turns them into a face print: a list of numbers that describes your face, from which the photo can't be rebuilt. Your selfies and face print are stored on our servers.
  • Photos you share. When you share a photo with a friend, we store a small preview and the original file, including the date, location and camera details saved in it, so your friend gets the same photo you took.
  • Connections and invites. We store who you're connected to and the phone numbers of people you invite.
  • Crash reports. If Overlap crashes, it sends us technical details (your device model, iOS version, app version and what the app was doing) so we can fix it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your photo library. Overlap looks through your photos on your phone to find faces and group them by person. That index of faces and people stays on your phone and isn't uploaded.
  • Your contacts. Overlap uses your contacts on your phone to help you name the people in your photos. To show which of them already use Overlap, it checks their phone numbers against Overlap accounts. Those numbers aren't stored, except for people you invite.

How your information is used and shared

  • With friends you connect with. When you and a friend connect, each of you can see the other's name and phone number, and your face print is made available to their copy of Overlap, so their phone can find you in their photos. Photos either of you shares are visible only to the two of you.
  • With people who have your number. Someone who has your phone number in their contacts can see that you're on Overlap, and your display name.
  • With our service providers. Our database, file storage and sign-in run on Supabase, and phone verification texts are sent through Supabase's text-message provider. They process data only to run Overlap for us.
  • Never for advertising. We don't sell your information, use it for ads, or share it with data brokers. Face data is used only to find you in your friends' photos.
  • When the law requires it. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of Overlap's users.

Your choices

  • Review before sharing, or auto-share. You choose, for each friend, whether to review photos before they're sent or let new photos of them go automatically.
  • Take a photo back. You can unshare a photo you sent at any time until your friend saves it to their own Photos library. After that, it's theirs.
  • Permissions. You can turn off Overlap's access to your camera, photos or contacts at any time in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Delete your account. In Overlap, go to Settings, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your selfies and face print, your connections, every photo you've shared, and your access to photos friends sent you, and erases Overlap's data on your phone. Photos you already saved to your Photos library stay there, and so do photos your friends saved.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account. Shared photos are deleted when they're unshared or when the sender deletes their account. Crash reports don't contain your photos, and we keep them only as long as we need them to fix problems.

Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access rules, so each person can reach only their own data and what friends have shared with them. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you about any breach that affects it, as the law requires.

Children

Overlap isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
